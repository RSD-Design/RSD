COMMON QUESTIONS

What clients often want to know.

What types of solicitations do you support?

Support can include RFPs, RFQs, RFIs and related government or public-sector solicitations, depending on the opportunity and scope.

Do you guarantee contract awards?

No. Proposal consulting can improve preparation, compliance and execution quality, but award decisions remain with the buyer and depend on many factors outside any consultant’s control.
